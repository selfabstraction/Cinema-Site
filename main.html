Wolf Cinema: House Rules
Welcome to Wolf Cinema, where movie magic meets the spirit of the pack. To ensure every guest enjoys an immersive, comfortable, and unforgettable cinematic experience, we ask everyone to respect our 12 simple rules:

1. Silence Your Devices
Please switch all mobile phones, tablets, and smartwatches to silent or airplane mode before the feature presentation begins. The glow and chime of a screen disrupt the immersion for your fellow moviegoers.
2. No Flash Photography or Recording
Piracy and filming are strictly prohibited. Recording, streaming, or taking flash photos of the screen at any point during trailers or the feature film is illegal and will result in immediate removal from the premises.
3. Keep the Pack Quiet
Once the movie starts, please refrain from talking or whispering. If you need to discuss the plot twists, save your excitement for the credits or the lobby!
4. Respect Personal Space and Seating
Please sit only in your assigned seat as indicated on your ticket. Keep your feet off the upholstery and avoid kicking the seats in front of you.
5. Arrive Promptly
Trailers and pre-show content run on a strict schedule. We recommend arriving 15 minutes before showtime to find your seat, settle in, and grab your snacks without disturbing others.
6. Late Entry Policy
To maintain a distraction-free environment, late arrivals may not be permitted into the auditorium once the main feature has begun, depending on the film. Plan your travel accordingly!
7. Clean Up After Yourself
We take pride in keeping our dens pristine. When the credits roll, please dispose of your empty popcorn tubs, cups, and wrappers in the bins provided at the exit doors.
8. Age Restrictions are Strictly Enforced
For R-rated, NC-17, or age-restricted features, valid government-issued photo ID is required at the door. No exceptions will be made, so please be prepared to show identification.
9. Outside Food and Beverages
Only snacks and drinks purchased at the Wolf Cinema concession stand are permitted inside the auditoriums. Please finish any outside food before entering.
10. Appropriate Attire
Shirts and shoes must be worn at all times while inside the cinema building. Costumes are welcome for special events, provided they do not obscure the vision or safety of other guests.
11. Zero Tolerance for Disruptive Behavior
Wolf Cinema maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, unruly behavior, intoxication, or disrespect toward staff and fellow patrons. Violators will be escorted out without a refund.
12. Respect the Pack (Staff and Patrons)
Above all, treat everyone with kindness and respect. Our team is here to ensure you have a fantastic time—if you need assistance, don't hesitate to flag down a crew member.

Welcome to Wolf Cinema.

New York's boldest destination for independent film and cinematic culture. Located in the heart of NYC, Wolf Cinema is where groundbreaking independent films, restored classics, and midnight cult favorites find a home. Enjoy cutting-edge theaters featuring pristine 4K laser projection and immersive spatial audio from plush, custom-designed seating. Grab locally crafted New York beers, artisan wines, and gourmet snacks at The Wolf Den Bar before or after your show, and join us for exclusive director Q&As, live-scored retrospectives, and late-night screenings.
